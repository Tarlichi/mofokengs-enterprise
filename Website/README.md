## Project Overview

Mofokeng's Enterprise is a community-driven organisation based in Uitenhage that focuses on reducing unemployment through job placement and skills development. This website serves as a professional online platform to connect job seekers with local employment opportunities.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

- Reduce unemployment in Uitenhage
- Connect job seekers with local employers
- Provide skills training and CV assistance
- Create a professional online presence
- Attract corporate partners and sponsors

---

## Key Features and Functionality

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Home | Hero section, services preview, latest news, locations |
| About Us | History, mission, vision, impact stats, team members |
| Services | Detailed descriptions of all services offered |
| Enquiry | Form for job seekers, employers, volunteers, and sponsors |
| Contact | Contact details, office hours, contact form, map placeholder |
