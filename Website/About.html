About Us

Learn about our mission, vision, and the impact we're making in Uitenhage.

Our Story

Mofokeng's Enterprise was founded in 2017 by Relebohile Mofokeng, a local entrepreneur who recognised the urgent need for a structured platform to connect skilled and unskilled workers with employment opportunities in Uitenhage.

Coming from a not-so-privileged background filled with crime, poverty, and theft, a need for change was required. The lack of government assistance led to an increase of economic issues in the town and setbacks on community improvement programs. These setbacks called for a change, which is where Mofokeng's Enterprise came in.

By providing employment and medical care with the help of government funding, and hiring security to reduce the number of incidents that occur on a regular basis, the enterprise has improved the economic standing of the town.

Our Mission

To reduce the unemployment rate by 20% in Uitenhage by providing accessible job placement services, skills training, and ongoing support to both job seekers and local businesses.

Our Vision

To become a renowned enterprise which changes the lives of thousands of civilians by creating a self-sustaining community where every resident has access to meaningful employment opportunities.
