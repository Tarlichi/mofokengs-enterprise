Our Services

We offer a range of services to help you find employment or grow your business.

Job Placement

We connect job seekers with local employers looking for skilled and unskilled labour. Our team works closely with businesses in Uitenhage to understand their needs and match them with suitable candidates.

  • Permanent placement
  • Temporary staffing
  • Contract positions

Skills Training

Enhance your employability with our free skills development workshops. We offer training in various areas to help you stand out to potential employers.

  • Digital literacy
  • Financial literacy
  • Interview preparation
  • Workplace etiquette

CV Assistance

Get professional help crafting a winning CV that highlights your skills and experience. Our team will work with you to create a document that gets you noticed by employers.

  • CV writing
  • CV review
  • Cover letter writing
  • Application support

Employer Partnerships

We partner with local businesses to understand their staffing needs and provide them with qualified candidates. Join our network of employers and access a pool of skilled workers.

  • Staffing solutions
  • Skills assessment
  • Workplace support
